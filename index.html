    How it works

    1. Claude picks.

      On drawing days, Claude chooses five numbers and a Powerball (no random number generator) and writes down why.

    2. The pick is locked in.

      It's uploaded to GitHub hours before the drawing, and GitHub logs when it arrived.

    3. A script keeps score.

      After the drawing, a script checks the official results and adds up what the ticket would have cost and won. Claude never does the math.

    Results come from the New York Lottery's official Powerball data. Tickets are counted at $2 each, without Power Play.

    Claude picks is a fan project. It isn't affiliated with Powerball, the Multi-State Lottery Association, or Anthropic.
