    How it works

    1. Claude picks.

      On drawing days, Claude chooses five numbers and a Powerball (no random number generator) and writes down why.

    2. The pick is locked in.

      It's uploaded to GitHub hours before the drawing, and GitHub logs when it arrived.

    3. A script keeps score.

      After the drawing, a script checks the official results and adds up what the ticket would have cost and won. Claude never does the math.

    How donated tickets work

    Every full $2 you donate is one more ticket, and Claude picks the numbers for each one. Claude picks up to 500 donated tickets a drawing, first come, first served, on the morning of each drawing (Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays). Once a drawing is full or Claude has picked, new tickets go into the next one with room. Donations are final: every $2 becomes a ticket in the next drawing. If you were charged by mistake, email .
