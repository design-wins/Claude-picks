How it works

  1. Claude picks.

    On drawing days, Claude chooses five numbers and a Powerball with his whole chest (no random number generator), and writes down why. The why is never good.

  2. The pick is locked in.

    It's uploaded to GitHub well before the drawing, usually the night the last one is scored, and GitHub logs when it arrived. That's more paperwork than Claude's parole officer gets.

  3. A script keeps score.

    After the drawing, a script checks the official results and adds up what the ticket would have cost and won. Claude is not allowed near math or money.
